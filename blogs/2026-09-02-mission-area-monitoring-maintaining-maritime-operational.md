In Q2 2026, Windward recorded 3,137 prolonged dark activity events involving 2,157 unique cargo ships and tankers over 10,000 DWT, and 171,286 distinct vessels affected by GPS jamming at least once. None of those figures describe a list of vessels. They describe conditions inside specific stretches of water.
