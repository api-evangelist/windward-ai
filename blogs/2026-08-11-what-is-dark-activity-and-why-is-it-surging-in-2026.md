At a Glance What Is Dark Activity? Dark activity refers to periods when a commercial vessel intentionally stops broadcasting its Automatic Identification System, or AIS, signal. When a vessel goes dark, its position, course, speed, and identity are no longer visible to the shore-based and satellite-based tracking systems that comprise the global maritime picture.
